README: Power-BI-Projects
Chicago Crime Dataset Overview
This dataset explores reported crimes in the City of Chicago, offering a detailed look at offense types, locations, arrest patterns, and long‑term trends. It is commonly used for predictive modeling, geospatial analysis, and public‑policy research.
README: Healthcare Insurance Providers Dataset Overview
This dataset summarizes healthcare insurance providers, coverage types, and availability across regions. It is useful for analyzing market competition, accessibility, and consumer options within the U.S. healthcare system.
README: Porch Piracy in the U.S. and California Overview
This dataset examines package theft (“porch piracy”) across the United States, with a focused subset on California. It includes incident counts, survey‑based victimization rates, and contextual factors such as population density and delivery volume.
